The growing- and foraging-related pages assume the northeastern United
States, and New Hampshire in particular: a short frost-free growing season,
cold-hardy crop and storage considerations, and a set of regional wild edible
species and their dangerous lookalikes. Where a claim is safety-critical —
plant identification, canning acidity, curing salt — the pages say so
explicitly and point to the primary source rather than asserting it from
memory.
