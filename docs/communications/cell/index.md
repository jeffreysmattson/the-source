# Cell

Cellular phone and data service: the communications method most people
already own and know how to use, but the least resilient of the options in
[Communications](../index.md) — it depends on the carrier's tower network,
backhaul, and grid power all staying up.

Pages will be added here covering signal boosters and repeaters, keeping a
phone charged and connected through a power outage, and the satellite
fallback messaging built into modern phones.
