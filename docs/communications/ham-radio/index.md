# Ham Radio

Amateur radio: the licensed service with the greatest range, flexibility,
and community infrastructure — repeaters, nets, emergency traffic handling —
of any option in [Communications](../index.md), at the cost of a licensing
exam.

Where [GMRS](../gmrs/index.md) is one license covering a fixed set of channels
and power levels, ham radio opens every band from 160 m to microwave and
above, with modes ranging from voice to digital data to satellite, limited
only by your license class and the rules of each band. The entry barrier is
the exam: three tiers (Technician, General, Amateur Extra), each a multiple-
choice test drawn from a public pool, taken at a Volunteer Examiner session
that meets roughly monthly in most New England counties.
