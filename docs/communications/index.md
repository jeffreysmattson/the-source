# Communications

Staying in contact, and staying informed, when phone networks and the
internet are unavailable or overloaded.

- [Ham Radio](ham-radio/index.md) — licensed amateur radio: long range,
  repeater networks, and emergency traffic handling
- [GMRS](gmrs/index.md) — license-by-household radio for family and
  small-group communications
- [Cell](cell/index.md) — cellular phone and data, and keeping it working
  through an outage
- [Satellite Internet](satellite-internet/index.md) — broadband terminals,
  satellite messengers, and satellite phones

Ham radio and GMRS require an FCC license to transmit legally in the United
States; neither requires one to listen. Cell and satellite service are
licensed at the carrier level, not the user's. Where a page assumes a
particular license class or region, it says so.
