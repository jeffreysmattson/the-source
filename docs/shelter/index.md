# Shelter

Insulation, heating, repair, and keeping a structure habitable.

This section covers the adjacent question to [Building](../building/index.md):
not how to construct a structure, but how to keep one — or a field-expedient
substitute for one — warm, dry, and heatable when normal help and normal
supply chains are not available.

- [Emergency Shelter Construction](10-emergency-shelter-construction.md)
- [Insulating an Existing Structure](20-insulating-existing-structure.md)
- [Wood Stove Installation and Clearances](30-wood-stove-installation-and-clearances.md)
- [Roof and Window Repair Without Supply Chain Access](40-roof-and-window-repair-without-supply-chain.md)

The pages assume the northeastern United States, the same regional context as
the Building section: deep winter cold, heavy snow load, and a construction
and heating season shaped by both.
