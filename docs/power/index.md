# Power

Generation, storage, wiring, and fuel for keeping the lights and the
heating-system controls on when utility power isn't available.

Solar and generators are the two ways this section gets you electricity;
batteries store it, and DC wiring is the common thread connecting a solar
array, a battery bank, and the loads they feed. Read the DC wiring page once
for the concepts even if you're only building one of the other three — the
solar and battery pages both point back to it for wire-gauge and fusing
detail rather than repeating it.

- [Solar Panel Sizing and Installation](10-solar-sizing.md)
- [Battery Bank Design and Maintenance](20-battery-banks.md)
- [Generator Operation and Fuel Storage](30-generators.md)
- [Basic DC Wiring and Safety](40-dc-wiring-safety.md)

The pages assume a fixed dwelling in the northeastern United States, and New
Hampshire in particular: cold-weather effects on batteries and fuel, heavy
snow load on solar racking, and winter storm outages as the primary reason
this section exists. Where a number is set locally — snow load, permitting,
fire-code adoption — the pages say so rather than printing a figure that looks
authoritative and might be wrong for your town.
